S08「AI 與人：8 萬人的真心話」— 8 張配圖 v1 vs v2 比對

升級方法：攝影機+鏡頭規格 / 膠片模擬+調色LUT / 物理光學細節 / 導演風格引用

Prompt 升級方法論 — 5 個維度

1
攝影機 + 鏡頭規格

ARRI Alexa 65 / RED V-Raptor + Panavision Ultra Vista / Cooke S7/i — 指定焦距、光圈 T-stop、景深特性

2
膠片模擬 + 調色 LUT

Kodak Vision3 500T / Fujifilm Eterna / Ektachrome — 指定 push/pull stops、色調偏移、grain 特性

3
物理光學細節

Tyndall 散射、焦散光紋 (caustics)、色散 (chromatic aberration)、anamorphic 橢圓散景、體積光粒子

4
材質物理描述

指定物體表面特性 — 銅鍋反射率、棉布織紋、混凝土骨材紋理、雨滴表面張力

5
導演 / 攝影師風格錨點

Roger Deakins + Emmanuel Lubezki + Wong Kar-wai — 每張指定 2-3 個風格交叉引用

1/8 — Cover 封面：群眾仰望光芒

Crowd + Crepuscular Light + Brutalist Architecture
v1 — 基礎 Prompt
v2 — 電影海報級
Cinematic wide shot, 16:9. A vast crowd... anamorphic lens flare. Color grading: teal shadows with warm amber highlights, inspired by Blade Runner 2049. Photorealistic, 8K.
Shot on ARRI Alexa 65 with Panavision Ultra Vista 40mm, T2.8... Kodak Vision3 500T 5219 pushed one stop — deep teal-cyan shadows (#1a3a4a), halated warm highlights bleeding at edges. Fine film grain ISO 500. Chromatic aberration at frame edges. Ref: Lubezki + Deakins + Villeneuve.

2/8 — Cooking 母女廚房

Golden Hour Backlight + Steam Tyndall + Copper Pot
v1 — 基礎 Prompt
v2 — 電影海報級
Cinematic golden hour photograph, 16:9. A parent holding child's hand... Warm backlit silhouette with lens flare. Color palette: rich warm amber, honey gold.
Shot on ARRI Alexa Mini LF with Cooke S7/i 32mm, T2.0... steam catches golden backlight creating Tyndall scattering. Copper pot with hand-blown glass caustic patterns. Fujifilm Eterna Vivid 500D. Ref: Ang Lee's "Eat Drink Man Woman" + Terrence Malick.

3/8 — Triptych 三聯畫

Three Color Worlds + Material Texture + Emotional Lighting
v1 — 基礎 Prompt
v2 — 電影海報級
Cinematic triptych, 16:9, three vertical panels. LEFT: soldier in bunker, blue glow. CENTER: elderly man with laptop, warm lamp. RIGHT: young woman at computer, rain window.
Shot on RED V-Raptor 8K with Zeiss Supreme Prime 35mm, T1.5. Dog tags catching glint. Grandfather's gnarled arthritic hands with visible tremor. Ceramic filament bulb, mended elbow patch. Crumpled energy drink can. Kodak 5219 500T pushed two stops. Ref: Iñárritu + Wong Kar-wai + Fincher.

4/8 — Light-Shadow 光影分界

Volumetric Dust Motes + Penumbra Gradient + Cross-Process
v1 — 基礎 Prompt
v2 — 電影海報級
Cinematic medium shot, 16:9. A person at boundary of warm light and cold shadow in cathedral-like space. Left: golden volumetric light. Right: deep blue-black shadow. Long shadow connects both.
Shot on ARRI Alexa 65 with Panavision C-Series 50mm, T2.3. Dust motes with diffraction rings. Caustic light from imperfect glass. 30cm penumbra gradient. Kodak Ektachrome warm + Fujifilm Provia cool cross-processed at boundary. Ref: Deakins + Kamiński + Vittorio Storaro.

5/8 — Rescue 深夜求助

Caravaggio Chiaroscuro + Rain Bokeh + Skin Detail
v1 — 基礎 Prompt
v2 — 電影海報級
Cinematic intimate shot, 16:9. A young man on bed at 3 AM, holding phone close to chest. Relief mixed with exhaustion, tears. Warm amber phone light, deep dark blue shadows. Rain window.
Shot on ARRI Alexa Mini with Leica Summilux-C 29mm, T1.4. Tear tracks glistening on stubbled cheeks. Rain — each raindrop individually resolved as tiny lens creating inverted bokeh. Caravaggio-like chiaroscuro uplight, 4:1 contrast ratio. Kodak 250D cross-processed with 500T, ISO 1600 pushed. Ref: Xavier Dolan + Park Chan-wook + Barry Jenkins.

6/8 — Risk 深夜筆電

Color Temperature Boundary + Anamorphic Bokeh + Sheet Texture
v1 — 基礎 Prompt
v2 — 電影海報級
Cinematic medium shot, 16:9. Person in bed at night, face lit by laptop, partner sleeping with back turned. Cool blue vs warm distant partner. Emotional infidelity with technology.
Shot on Sony Venice 2 with Cooke Anamorphic/i SF 50mm, T2.3. 400-thread-count cotton weave texture. Color temperature boundary on sheets between them is the visual thesis. Phone notification pulsing faint green dot. Fujifilm Eterna 500T. Ref: Wong Kar-wai + Sofia Coppola + David Fincher.

7/8 — East-West 東西對比

Wabi-sabi vs Techno-optimism + Split Color Grade + Motion Blur
v1 — 基礎 Prompt
v2 — 電影海報級
Cinematic split-screen, 16:9, vertical center divide. LEFT: East Asian person in minimalist room, paper lantern, warm tones. RIGHT: soaring atrium, dozens of people, cool LED light, geometric patterns.
Shot on ARRI Alexa 65 with Panavision Ultra Vista 65mm. LEFT: washi paper fiber texture shadows on tatami. Individually resolved pine needles on bonsai. RIGHT: 1/15s motion blur on people = relentless velocity. Geodesic dome structure. LEFT: Fujifilm Velvia 50, RIGHT: bleach bypass. Ref: Yasujirō Ozu + Christopher Nolan + Ridley Scott.

8/8 — Crossroads 岔路抉擇

Wildflower Species + Volumetric God Rays + Aurora Voronoi
v1 — 基礎 Prompt
v2 — 電影海報級
Cinematic aerial wide, 16:9, epic landscape. Lone figure at crossroads. LEFT: golden sunrise, lush wildflowers, god rays. RIGHT: deep blue luminous fog, geometric aurora. Hope vs uncertainty.
Shot on ARRI Alexa 65 with Panavision System 65 24mm ultra-wide. Visible species: lupines, poppies, buttercups with dew specular highlights showing surface tension. RIGHT: obsidian-like path surface reflecting aurora, Voronoi light tessellations. LEFT: Kodak Ektachrome 100 VS, RIGHT: Fujifilm Provia pushed two stops. Ref: Malick + Villeneuve + Tarkovsky.
